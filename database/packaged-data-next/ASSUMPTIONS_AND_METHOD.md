# Versioned synthetic AquaTrack dataset

This is a **fictional demonstration scenario**, not records supplied by a Nairobi water producer. `generate_dataset.py` is the complete source of the records. Seed `20261105` makes regeneration deterministic. The exact CSV hashes and record counts are in `dataset_metadata.json`.

## Company and calendar

The company operates Monday to Saturday from **1 January 2024 through 5 November 2026**, inclusive. Sundays are closed. The demand table has four rows per calendar day so each closed Sunday is explicitly labelled `closed_sunday` with zero accepted orders; an operating day without accepted orders is labelled `operating_zero_orders`. No operational transaction is dated on a Sunday. The 7-day forecast is the next Monday–Sunday calendar week and the exploratory 30-day forecast is 30 calendar days. Both include closed Sundays as known zeros. They are not seven or thirty operating-day forecasts.

The four existing product IDs represent 500 mL (24 bottles/case), 1 L (12), 5 L (4), and 20 L (1). Current displayed prices are KSh 620, 540, 720, and 300 per case respectively. Historical order line prices use a simple fictional 5% lower 2024 price and 2% lower 2025 price; every line retains its own price snapshot. These are assumptions, not observed price histories.

There are 32 fictional customers in retail, restaurants, hotels, schools, offices, hospitals, supermarkets, and residential estates. The scenario assigns daily, several-times-weekly, weekly, fortnightly, or occasional ordering patterns with customer-specific frequency and size factors. Product mix differs by customer category. Weekday, Saturday, school-term proxy, month, category and product interact. School-term months and monthly seasonality are **synthetic proxies**, not measured school calendars, rainfall or weather. A moderate non-linear growth factor, an April–May 2025 slowdown, ordinary noise, rare unusually large orders, cancellations and production disruptions add variation. The explicit formulas are in `generate_dataset.py`; no target is generated by a fitted ML model. Random Forest was **not** forced to win.

## Operational and financial relationships

Production uses the policy effective on its date. The policy's 180-day shelf life is a scenario assumption only, **not a certified standard**. Costs are per case and a batch stores the historical total and cost per saleable case. A small fraction of batches has rejected cases. Most batches are released on the same day using simulated QA references; that is a simplification, not evidence of real laboratory tests. The exception examples include a current quality hold, failed-check quarantines, a recall with no dispatch, and one documented disposal after expiry. `batch_quality_events.csv` records their status transitions. Production adds physical stock; rejection, dispatch and disposal subtract stock. The simulator checks availability before accepting a complete order, then dispatches using earliest-expiring released stock. Draft and cancelled requests do not reserve or deduct stock.

At most one company-truck trip per operating day contains several complete orders. `delivery_attempts.csv` includes rare failed stops followed by successful retry on the same trip, with a single dispatch, stock deduction and invoice for each order. One order on the final date has a failed attempt still open: its order, dispatch and trip remain `dispatched`, and it has no invoice or payment until delivery is confirmed. This matches the application's current dispatched state; it does not invent a `failed` status that AquaTrack does not support. A trip's fuel and other expense are separate from the fixed delivery-zone fee charged once to each customer order. Invoices equal the historical order total. Some invoices are unpaid or partially paid; payment is not a second sale.

`daily_product_demand.csv` is derived from **accepted dispatched or delivered orders**, by order date and product, and is the ML target used by the deployed application's eligible status filter. `cases_requested` includes draft and cancelled requests, so requested but unfulfilled demand is visible separately. `cases_ordered` must not be interpreted as every customer request or as a physical dispatch total. There are no missing observation dates in this synthetic set; explicit zero-order closures are in `daily_order_closures.csv`.

## Generated records and limits

See `dataset_metadata.json` for exact counts and SHA-256 hashes. In the current generation there are 4,160 dated demand rows, 8,698 customer orders, 1,173 production batches, 887 truck trips, 8,656 dispatches and 8,655 invoices. There are 34 failed delivery attempts: 33 were retried successfully and one remains open. The exceptions are deliberate, rare demonstration records; they are not evidence of real incident rates. Same-day production release, dispatch, delivery and invoicing are otherwise simplified. No returns or customer credits were simulated. Synthetic accuracy cannot establish real-world forecasting accuracy.

Run `python generate_dataset.py`, then `python validate_dataset.py`. The validator reconciles transactions and loads the relationships into a separate in-memory SQLite database with foreign-key checks. It also regenerates the CSVs in a temporary folder under this workspace and compares every SHA-256 hash. It does **not** load the Laravel MySQL schema or modify the live database.
